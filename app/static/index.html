Historical demonstration: Recommendations use previously collected Bangkok taxi data, not live GPS feeds or real-time vacant-taxi counts. GPS only supplies your current position when your browser permits it.
Pickup probability and confidence are different: pickup probability is the model's estimated chance of a pickup within 15 minutes. Confidence describes how much supporting historical data is available for the recommended area.
